# Departure delays at New York airports in 2013

This project investigates how the percentage of flights departing at least
30 minutes late varied by scheduled departure hour at JFK, LaGuardia and
Newark in 2013. It also compares morning and evening flights under similar
weather conditions.

Data: flights and weather tables from the nycflights13 R package.

Flight data: https://nycflights13.tidyverse.org/reference/flights.html
Weather data: https://nycflights13.tidyverse.org/reference/weather.html
